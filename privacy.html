Blobboy Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Blobboy is a personal digital companion project designed to create an interactive character based on wellness information provided by the user's Oura account.

Information We Collect

With your authorization, Blobboy may access information from the Oura API, including daily wellness information such as:

  • Sleep information and sleep-related scores
  • Daily activity information and activity scores
  • Readiness information and readiness scores

Blobboy only requests access to the Oura information necessary to provide its intended functionality.

How We Use Your Information

Oura data is used to personalize the Blobboy experience. For example, sleep, activity, and readiness information may influence Blobboy's appearance, behavior, animations, environment, or other interactive elements.

Blobboy does not use Oura information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or the sale of personal information.

Data Sharing

Blobboy does not sell, rent, or share your Oura data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Blobboy is currently being developed as a personal project. Oura data will only be stored when necessary to provide or improve the functionality of the application.

Authentication credentials and access tokens will be handled separately from the public Blobboy website and will not be intentionally exposed through the application's public source code.

Oura API

Blobboy uses the Oura API to access authorized wellness information. By connecting an Oura account, you authorize Blobboy to access the information requested during the authorization process.

Blobboy is not affiliated with or operated by Oura Health Ltd.

Data Retention and Deletion

You may disconnect your Oura account from Blobboy at any time. If Blobboy stores personal data associated with your account, you may request that such data be deleted.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Blobboy develops and its functionality changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact the Blobboy project owner through the contact information associated with this project.
